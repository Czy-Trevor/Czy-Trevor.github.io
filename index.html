Short Bio

I am an M.Eng. student in Intelligent Science and Technology at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST), advised by Prof. Dongrui Wu. I study how neural decoders can remain reliable when they meet a new person, task, or deployment condition.

My current work connects transferable EEG decoding for practical brain–computer interfaces with cross-subject neural–visual representation learning for brain-to-image retrieval.

Research Interests: transferable neural decoding, cross-subject learning, multimodal representation learning, and efficient adaptation.

Zhenyao Cui, Siyang Li, Siyuan Kan, Yudi Wang, Fei Luo, Dongrui Wu

TBME · Under Review

MCL-Align: Marginal–Conditional–Label Alignment for Cross-Subject EEG Regression

Zhenyao Cui, Jiahui Chai, Siyuan Kan, Yudi Wang, Dongrui Wu

IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering, under review

Short Summary: Cross-subject EEG regression shifts in three places at once: the signal distribution, the feature–label relationship, and each subject’s label scale. MCL-Align handles all three with marginal alignment, conditional-direction matching, and label standardization.

Zhenyao Cui, Siyuan Kan, Siyang Li, Ziwei Wang, Dongrui Wu

Manuscript under review

Short Summary: EEG–image relations can be consistent across people while appearing in different subject-specific coordinates. SCORE recovers the coordinates of an unseen subject without target labels, source EEG at deployment, or encoder updates.
