About

I am an M.Eng. student in Intelligent Science and Technology at HUST, advised by Prof. Dongrui Wu. I study how neural decoders can remain reliable when they meet a new person, task, or deployment condition.

My current work connects two problems: transferable EEG decoding for practical brain–computer interfaces, and cross-subject neural–visual representation learning for brain-to-image retrieval.

Research interests: transferable neural decoding, cross-subject learning, multimodal representation learning, and efficient adaptation.

IEEE TETCI · 2026

A Fuzzy Set Based Classification-to-Regression Extension Framework for Transfer Learning and Its Application to Brain-Computer Interfaces

Zhenyao Cui, Siyang Li, Siyuan Kan, Yudi Wang, Fei Luo, Dongrui Wu

Most transfer-learning methods for BCI regression inherit tools designed for classification, where labels are discrete. C2R replaces hard classes with fuzzy classes over continuous targets, making conditional alignment usable for regression.

IEEE TBME · Under review

MCL-Align: Marginal–Conditional–Label Alignment for Cross-Subject EEG Regression

Zhenyao Cui, Jiahui Chai, Siyuan Kan, Yudi Wang, Dongrui Wu

Cross-subject EEG regression shifts in three places at once: the signal distribution, the feature–label relationship, and each subject’s label scale. MCL-Align handles all three with marginal alignment, conditional-direction matching, and label standardization.

Manuscript under review

SCORE: Subject Coordinate Recovery for Label-Free Cross-Subject EEG-to-Image Retrieval

Zhenyao Cui, Siyuan Kan, Siyang Li, Ziwei Wang, Dongrui Wu

EEG–image relations can be consistent across people while appearing in different subject-specific coordinates. SCORE recovers the coordinates of an unseen subject without target labels, source EEG at deployment, or encoder updates.

Beyond Trial Averaging: Anchoring Neural and Visual Representations for Few-Repetition Brain-to-Image Retrieval

Zhenyao Cui, Siyuan Kan, Dingkun Liu, Dongrui Wu

Few-repetition neural retrieval fails for more than noisy queries: the image gallery can also be misaligned. NEAR uses a stable high-repetition neural representation as a common anchor for both the neural query and the visual gallery.

Backpropagation-Free Test-Time Adaptation for Lightweight EEG-Based Brain-Computer Interfaces

Siyang Li†, Jiayi Ouyang†, Zhenyao Cui†, Ziwei Wang, Tianwang Jia, Feng Wan, Dongrui Wu

Test-time adaptation is attractive for streaming BCIs, but most methods update model weights and assume white-box access. BFT adapts each EEG trial using forward-only, reliability-weighted transformations for lightweight and quantized deployment.

National Science Review · Accepted

EEG-FM-Compass: Progress, Benchmarking, and Future Directions for EEG Foundation Models

Dingkun Liu, Yuheng Chen, Zhu Chen, Zhenyao Cui, Yaozhi Wen, Jiayu An, Jingwei Luo, Dongrui Wu

A survey and controlled benchmark of EEG foundation models. The study organizes 55 models, evaluates 12 open-source models across 13 datasets, and examines whether scale and common adaptation protocols improve cross-subject generalization and few-shot calibration.

CMCRD: Cross-Modal Contrastive Representation Distillation for Emotion Recognition

Siyuan Kan, Huanyu Wu, Zhenyao Cui, Fan Huang, Xiaolong Xu, Dongrui Wu

CMCRD uses a stronger auxiliary modality during training while keeping inference unimodal. Contrastive representation distillation transfers complementary information between EEG and eye movements without requiring both sensors at test time.
