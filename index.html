Short Bio

I am an M.Eng. student in Intelligent Science and Technology at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST). I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Dongrui Wu, an IEEE Fellow and the Editor-in-Chief of IEEE Transactions on Fuzzy Systems.

My research broadly spans machine learning, neural decoding and brain–computer interfaces, multimodal and cross-modal learning, and transfer learning. I am especially interested in models that generalize across people, modalities, and changing data conditions.

I expect to graduate in June 2027 and am seeking Ph.D. opportunities starting in Fall 2027.

Research Interests: neural decoding, machine learning, multimodal and cross-modal learning, and transfer learning.

Current Research

My current research is organized around two connected directions:

🧠Transferable Neural Decoding and Brain–Computer Interfaces

Neural decoding models must cope with large subject-to-subject variation, continuous targets, limited calibration data, and practical deployment constraints. I study transfer learning and test-time adaptation methods that make EEG decoding more reliable across users and conditions.

Representative work: C2R (IEEE TETCI 2026) extends class-conditional transfer to continuous regression with fuzzy sets and a target-domain risk bound; MCL-Align (IEEE TBME, under review) jointly aligns marginal, conditional, and label distributions; and BFT (IEEE JBHI 2026) combines data augmentation, Monte Carlo-dropout-inspired feature masking, and learning-to-rank to reduce test-time uncertainty without backpropagation.

🔗Multimodal and Cross-Modal Neural Representation Learning

I study how neural representations connect to images and other modalities, especially when subject-specific geometry and noisy, few-repetition signals disrupt cross-modal alignment.

Representative work: SCORE identifies systematic cross-subject coordinate differences and recovers an unseen subject’s coordinates through reliable EEG–image anchors and orthogonal reorientation; NEAR identifies a non-transitive alignment pattern under few repetitions and uses the high-repetition neural center to anchor both queries and gallery images.

TETCI 2026

A Fuzzy Set Based Classification-to-Regression Extension Framework for Transfer Learning and Its Application to Brain-Computer Interfaces

Zhenyao Cui, Siyang Li, Siyuan Kan, Yudi Wang, Fei Luo, Dongrui Wu

IEEE Transactions on Emerging Topics in Computational Intelligence, 2026

C2R extends class-conditional transfer learning from classification to continuous EEG regression by replacing hard classes with fuzzy class memberships. It also derives a target-domain transfer-learning error bound based on fuzzy class-conditional probabilities.

arXiv 2026

SCORE: Subject Coordinate Recovery for Label-Free Cross-Subject EEG-to-Image Retrieval

Zhenyao Cui, Siyuan Kan, Siyang Li, Ziwei Wang, Dongrui Wu

Manuscript under review · arXiv:2608.19134

In EEG-to-image retrieval, we find that subjects preserve similar relationships among concepts but express them along systematically different coordinate directions. SCORE selects reliable EEG–image landmarks and estimates an orthogonal reorientation to recover an unseen subject’s coordinates without target labels or source EEG at deployment.

arXiv 2026

Beyond Trial Averaging: Anchoring Neural and Visual Representations for Few-Repetition Brain-to-Image Retrieval

Zhenyao Cui, Siyuan Kan, Dingkun Liu, Dongrui Wu

Manuscript under review · arXiv:2608.19128

Under few repetitions, we identify a non-transitive pattern: the low-repetition neural query and the image representation each align with the high-repetition center, but not directly with each other. NEAR uses this center as a common anchor, denoising neural queries and predicting pseudo-anchors for gallery images across EEG, MEG, and fMRI.

TBME · Under Review

MCL-Align: Marginal–Conditional–Label Alignment for Cross-Subject EEG Regression

Zhenyao Cui, Jiahui Chai, Siyuan Kan, Yudi Wang, Dongrui Wu

IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering, under review

MCL-Align provides comprehensive probability alignment for cross-subject EEG regression. It jointly aligns marginal trial distributions, the direction of feature–label dependencies, and subject-specific label scale and offset through Euclidean Alignment, Conditional Direction Matching, and label standardization.

Manuscript available on request

JBHI · Accepted

Backpropagation-Free Test-Time Adaptation for Lightweight EEG-Based Brain-Computer Interfaces

Siyang Li†, Jiayi Ouyang†, Zhenyao Cui†, Ziwei Wang, Tianwang Jia, Feng Wan, Dongrui Wu

IEEE Journal of Biomedical and Health Informatics, accepted · Equal first author

BFT combines knowledge-guided EEG augmentation, Monte Carlo-dropout-inspired feature masking, and a source-trained learning-to-rank module. It ranks and aggregates transformed predictions to reduce instance-level uncertainty and improve transferability without test-time backpropagation, including for lightweight and quantized BCI models.

Dingkun Liu, Yuheng Chen, Zhu Chen, Zhenyao Cui, Yaozhi Wen, Jiayu An, Jingwei Luo, Dongrui Wu
