Research

Generalizable neural decoding, from signals to semantics.

My work focuses on the failure modes that appear when a neural decoder leaves its training subjects or recording conditions, then develops efficient alignment and adaptation methods to close that gap.

01 / RESEARCH LINE I

Generalizable EEG transfer and adaptation

C2R establishes a classification-to-regression transfer framework; MCL-Align extends it to marginal, conditional, and label alignment across unseen subjects; BFT then moves the line to backpropagation-free test-time adaptation for lightweight EEG interfaces.

TETCI Early Access · TBME under review · JBHI accepted
PaperCode
02 / RESEARCH LINE II

Cross-subject brain-to-image retrieval

SCORE first recovers subject-specific neural coordinates without target labels, source data, or encoder updates; NEAR then extends the line to few-repetition settings by anchoring noisy neural and visual representations.

Manuscripts under review · preprints coming soon

Projects

Systems built beyond the two research lines.

Selected engineering, competition, and industry-facing projects.

PROJECT & COMPETITION

EEG-controlled robotic arm

Designed the experimental paradigm, closed-loop control system, and EEG decoder for a robust, low-latency brain-to-robotic-arm interface. The system won the national championship at the 2025 China Brain-Computer Interface Competition.

INDUSTRY PROJECT

Multimodal intelligent cockpit evaluation

Led a Dongfeng Motor Group project spanning 29 evaluation dimensions, with EEG, speech, and camera data integrated into a unified scoring, visualization, and analysis pipeline.

About

Research with a systems instinct.

Background

I am an M.Eng. student in Intelligent Science and Technology at Huazhong University of Science and Technology, advised by Prof. Dongrui Wu. My interests include brain-computer interfaces, EEG decoding, transfer learning, neural representation learning, and efficient test-time adaptation.

I also led the design of an EEG-controlled robotic-arm system that won the national championship at the 2025 China Brain-Computer Interface Competition.
